# Jacob Cook's Security Engineering Portfolio

## Register
brand

## Users
Senior hiring managers at security-focused companies (Cloudflare, CrowdStrike, Zscaler, Cisco, Azure). Scanning 30 seconds on desktop, evaluating taste + rigor + technical depth.

## Brand Positioning
Detection engineer with obsessive attention to reducing alert noise and building reliable triage paths. Not flashy; not trendy. Editorial in tone, clinical in execution. The kind of engineer security teams trust to ship unattended.

## Product Purpose
Proof artifact: show detection logic reasoning, incident patterns, defense design thinking. The site itself IS a signal — how he thinks about clarity, precision, and editorial craft.

## Target Tone
- Authoritative, not promotional
- Precise, not flowery
- Composed, not excitable
- Technical, not jargony
- Restrained, not austere

## Anti-References
- Blue/purple "AI security" vibes (the reflexive Lila Ban)
- Centered hero with floating animation
- Gradient text, rounded-full CTAs with colored shadows
- Typewriter effects, emoji icons, "next-gen" copy
- 52 tailored resume PDFs as portfolio debt
- Card-based layouts with uniform grids
- Monochromatic blue palettes

## Content Principles
- Every word earns its place
- No em dashes
- Show outcomes, not just tools ("reduced alert noise by X%" vs "built SOAR")
- Sentence case everywhere
- Real metrics, real projects, no buzzwords

## Color Strategy
Restrained: warm bone canvas, one accent (terracotta). Single accent ≤10% surface. The anti-reflex move for security portfolios.
- Primary: Terracotta `#B8553E` for CTAs, active states, highlights
- No blues, no purples, no gradients

## Typography Strategy
Editorial precision through multi-typeface contrast.
- Display (headers): Cabinet Grotesk — character without trendiness
- Body (prose): Geist Sans — clean, restrained
- Mono (timestamps, certs, code): Geist Mono — credible, technical

## Motion Philosophy
Subtle, intentional. Spring physics on interaction (stiffness: 100, damping: 20). Fade-up on scroll entry. No auto-play, no cinematic flourishes. Perceived performance > visual spectacle.

## Scene Sentence
A senior security hiring manager scanning Jacob's portfolio on a 14-inch MacBook at midday, between five other candidates, looking for evidence of taste and rigor.

**Derived theme choice:** Light is the canonical default. Dark mode is opt-in. Breaks the reflexive hacker-portfolio look.
